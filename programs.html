Program Overview

Reducing recidivism starts with a program communities can trust.

The Recidivism Reduction & Reentry Support Program is our flagship framework for state and federal partners — pairing data-driven strategy with dignified, human-centered service design. It's built to reduce repeat incarceration while giving returning citizens a real, supported path back into community life.

Our Key Messages

Evidence-Based Solutions

Holistic Reintegration

Housing, employment, and community support woven together — because successful reentry is never just one intervention.
